Mathematical Feature Engineering & Pipeline Design

Engineered Features:

  • TotalIncome: ApplicantIncome + CoapplicantIncome
    Rationale: Loans are serviced using combined household cash flow rather than single-applicant base salary.
  • Income_to_Loan_Ratio: TotalIncome / (LoanAmount + 0.001)
    Rationale: Assesses capital coverage relative to borrowed principal. Adding 0.001 prevents zero-division errors.

Anti-Leakage Preprocessing:

  • Numerical Pipeline: SimpleImputer(strategy='median') followed by StandardScaler().
  • Categorical Pipeline: SimpleImputer(strategy='most_frequent') followed by OneHotEncoder(drop='first').
  • Fit Isolation: Transformers are fitted exclusively on X_train and applied downstream to X_test and live inputs.

Faculty Viva Voce Preparation Guide

Top Exam Q&A
Q1: Why did you choose Logistic Regression as the best model instead of more complex models like SVM or Decision Trees?

Answer: On our holdout validation set, Logistic Regression achieved the highest F1-Score (90.81%) and overall accuracy (86.18%), with an exceptional 98.82% recall. In credit sanctioning, Logistic Regression provides stable probabilistic boundaries without overfitting small tabular datasets (614 rows).

Q2: What is Data Leakage and how did your implementation prevent it?

Answer: Data leakage occurs when information from outside the training dataset (such as test set statistics) is inadvertently used to transform features. We prevented this by isolating train_test_split before fitting the ColumnTransformer on X_train only. The saved pipeline is then used to transform test inputs and live user entries.

Q3: Why is F1-Score a better evaluation metric than Accuracy for this dataset?

Answer: The dataset has moderate class imbalance (~68.7% approved vs ~31.3% rejected). A naive baseline predicting all approvals would achieve ~68.7% accuracy while failing to detect credit risk. The F1-score balances precision and recall, ensuring both false approvals and false rejections are penalized.

Q4: What is the most critical feature in determining loan approval?

Answer: Credit History (Credit_History). Our exploratory count plots show that applicants meeting credit guidelines (1.0) have an approval rate exceeding 80%, while applicants without credit history (0.0) face near-total rejection.
