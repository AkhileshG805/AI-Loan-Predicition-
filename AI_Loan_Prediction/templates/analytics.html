Part 1: Classifier Performance & Confusion Matrix Analysis

Report Chapter 5.8 & 5.9

Visual comparisons of validation accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score across all 4 supervised classification models, alongside their respective confusion matrices.

Figure 5.1: Multi-Model Evaluation Comparison Bar Chart

Compares Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and F1-score across Logistic Regression, KNN, SVM, and Decision Tree. Logistic Regression leads with an F1-score of 90.81% and Accuracy of 86.18%.

Figure 5.2: Validation Confusion Matrices Grid

Visualizes True Positives, True Negatives, False Positives, and False Negatives for each model on the 123-sample holdout test set. Logistic Regression achieved 84 True Positives and 22 True Negatives with only 1 False Negative.

Part 2: Feature Relationships & Target Distribution

Report Chapter 5.6

Visualizing the statistical distributions that drive the model's decision boundaries.

Figure 5.3: Loan Approval Status by Credit History Flag

Confirms Credit History as the single highest statistical predictor. Applicants with Credit_History = 1.0 demonstrate an overwhelming sanction rate (~80%), whereas applicants with 0.0 are predominantly rejected.

Figure 5.4: Target Class Distribution (Approval vs Rejection)

Illustrates class distribution: 422 approved (68.7%) vs 192 rejected (31.3%). This imbalance underscores why stratified sampling and F1-Score optimization were mandatory.

Figure 5.5: Numerical Feature Correlation Heatmap

Pearson correlation matrix mapping linear relationships across ApplicantIncome, CoapplicantIncome, LoanAmount, Loan_Amount_Term, and Credit_History.

Figure 5.6: Loan Sanction Distribution by Property Area

Compares sanction volumes across Urban, Semiurban, and Rural regions. Semiurban applicants display higher approval proportions in the historical dataset.

Figure 5.7: Applicant Monthly Income Distribution

Displays a right-skewed distribution of applicant monthly earnings, demonstrating why median imputation and standard scaling are necessary for machine learning convergence.

Figure 5.8: Loan Sanction Rate by Education Level

Categorical comparison showing Graduate applicants achieve higher total approval volumes compared to non-graduates.
